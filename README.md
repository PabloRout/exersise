**Focus:** Hypertrophy • Unilateral Strength • Progressive Overload • Time Under Tension (3–4 sec eccentric)

---

## General Warm-up (Every Session)
- 40–60 push-ups (broken into sets as needed)
- 5–10 minutes of dynamic movements: arm circles, leg swings, bodyweight squats, jumping jacks, hip openers

## Progression Tips
- Prioritize a **3–4 second controlled lowering** on every repetition
- When you reach the top of the rep range with good form, increase reps, slow the tempo further, or add load
- Rest **60–90 seconds** between sets
- Track sets, reps, and RPE weekly

- After 4–6 weeks, increase load or slow the tempo further

# Complete Diet Plan for Hypertrophy (Muscle Gain)

**Daily Targets (61 kg):**
- Calories: **2500–2800 kcal**
- Protein: **120–140 g**
- Carbs: 280–350 g
- Fats: 60–80 g

### Sample Daily Structure (India-friendly)

**Meal 1 – Breakfast**  
3–4 eggs or 100–120 g paneer bhurji + 2 rotis + 1 glass milk  
≈ 35–40 g protein

**Meal 2 – Mid-Morning**  
1 banana + 20–25 g peanuts **or** 200 g curd + fruit  
≈ 12–18 g protein

**Meal 3 – Lunch**  
150–180 g rice or 3–4 rotis + dal / chicken / soya + sabzi + curd  
≈ 35–45 g protein

**Meal 4 – Pre-Workout**  
Banana + peanuts **or** 2 boiled eggs + 1 roti  
≈ 10–15 g protein

**Meal 5 – Post-Workout / Dinner**  
150–200 g chicken / paneer / soya + rice or rotis + vegetables + 1 tsp ghee  
≈ 40–50 g protein

**Meal 6 – Before Bed (optional)**  
200 ml milk or 150 g curd + peanut butter  
≈ 10–15 g protein

**Weekly Protein Sources:** Eggs, chicken, fish, paneer, soya chunks, dal, curd, milk, peanuts

**Daily Checklist:**
- 3.5–4.5 litres water
- Vegetables in at least 2 meals
- On Sunday you can reduce carbs slightly
