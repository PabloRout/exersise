# Improved 6-Day Full-Body Home Workout Plan (Limited Equipment)

**User Profile:** 21 Years Old | 61kg | 5'6"  
**Experience:** Intermediate (6+ Months)  
**Equipment:** Adjustable Dumbbells (10kg each + 4×3kg + 4×2kg), Pull-up Bar, Floor  
**Focus:** Hypertrophy, Unilateral Strength, Progressive Overload, Time Under Tension (3-4 sec eccentric phase)  

**General Warm-up (Every Session):**  
Perform 40-60 Push-ups (broken into sets as needed) followed by 5-10 minutes of dynamic movements (arm circles, leg swings, bodyweight squats, and jumping jacks).

**Progression Tips:**  
- Prioritize 3-4 second controlled lowering on every repetition.  
- Increase reps or slow the tempo when upper rep targets are achieved comfortably.  
- Use both 10kg dumbbells for bilateral movements; combine smaller plates for added load where possible.  
- Rest 60-90 seconds between sets. Track reps, sets, and perceived effort weekly.

---

## Weekly Schedule

- **Monday:** Full Body – Push Dominant  
- **Tuesday:** Full Body – Pull Dominant  
- **Wednesday:** Full Body – Legs & Core Dominant  
- **Thursday:** Full Body – Upper Body Volume  
- **Friday:** Full Body – Posterior Chain & Arms  
- **Saturday:** Full Body – Metabolic & Unilateral  
- **Sunday:** Complete Rest & Mobility

## Recovery & Nutrition Notes

- **Sunday:** Full rest. Include light mobility work or walking if desired.  
- Prioritize 7-9 hours of sleep nightly.  
- Consume 1.6-2.2g of protein per kg of body weight daily.  
- After 4-6 weeks, reassess and adjust volume or intensity for continued progress.

This plan ensures balanced full-body stimulus across six days while allowing Sunday recovery. It builds upon your previous routine with refined progression and exercise selection suited to your available equipment.
